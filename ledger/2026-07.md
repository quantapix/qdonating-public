## Anything broken or behind

**Behind (1) — this ledger is two days late, and that is a cadence miss.**
The July statement was due **2026-08-05** (five calendar days after month-end)
and posts **2026-08-07**. Under the drive's own cadence terms that breaks the
*trivially auditable* contract, so it is disclosed here as the first item rather
than folded into a footnote.

What broke has two parts, and the first one is not a scheduling failure.

*The first five days were externally gated.* Five documents were e-filed in
three state trial-court dockets on **2026-07-31**. One set was acted on the
same day; the other two were not resolved until **8/4** and **8/5**, and
resolving them took a call to the clerk's office on the afternoon of 8/5. A
federal filing whose record citations had to reflect that response was **held**
for it and mailed **2026-08-04** rather than at the start of that week. This
drive is one person; that arc ran 08-02 through 08-05 and consumed the window in
which the July statement was due. It is stated here as the plain timeline it is,
rather than described as an unspecified delay, because it is also the reason a
*reporting* obligation slipped: this drive's accountability artifacts and its
filings are produced by the same person.

*The second part is ours, and it is the one that is fixable.* The arc cleared on
8/5. The ledger still did not post on 8/6, when work sessions ran on other
subprojects. The monthly ledger has **no scheduled trigger of its own** — the
weekly digest does, which is why the weekly cadence has not slipped once, while
the monthly statement depends on a drive session happening to open inside the
five-day window. So an external gate cost the first five days, and the absence
of a trigger meant nothing caught the ledger in the two days after the gate
lifted. W31's § 6 had correctly flagged the 08-05 due date, but a note in a
digest is not a trigger: it can only fire inside a session that already exists.

What changed so it does not recur: the miss is tracked as an open item on this
subproject's next-steps list, with the fix scoped as a **scheduled trigger** in
the constellation's cron rather than another reminder, and the monthly-ledger
template now states that the ledger's own posting date is in scope for this
section. **No figure, event, or disclosure in this ledger was affected by the
delay** — the content is July's, and the two extra days bought no favourable
fact.
