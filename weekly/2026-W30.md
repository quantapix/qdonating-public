## 5. One reader question, answered

> *Seeded from the drive FAQ, tied to this week's § 1 note.* "The digest cites
> commit hashes I can't find in the public repos. Is that a discrepancy?"

No — it is the intended lag, and this week it is a wide one, so it is worth
stating plainly. The hashes in § 1 are **monorepo source** commits. The public
repos are redacted slices produced from that monorepo by the `/publish`
pipeline, which is a deliberate, reviewed step rather than a mirror that runs on
every commit — the redaction gate is what lets private litigation material stay
out of a public repo. When a refresh has not run, the public repos legitimately
sit behind the hashes cited here, and the digest says so (this week: § 6, the
full weekly refresh has not landed since W27). Each hash becomes independently
checkable in the mirror at the next refresh, and the digest is written in
advance of that push precisely so the claim can be checked against it
afterwards rather than being taken on trust. If a cited hash never appears after
a subsequent refresh, that is a reportable defect in this drive — say so in the
public Discussions.
