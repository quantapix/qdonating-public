## 5. One reader question, answered

> *Seeded (no inbound this week):* "§ 3 shows 57 new attack theorems. Did any
> of them break a proof?"

No. Each of the nine review rounds on 2026-11-23 ended with its judge recording
that no attack landed (§ 1). An attack theorem is a test: an adversary states
what a flawed proof would let through, and the round asks whether the proof
admits it. Removals are part of the same work: of the 19 theorem names
removed this week, 6 came back restated under the same name (§ 3).
