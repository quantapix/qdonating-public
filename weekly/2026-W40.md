## 5. One reader question, answered

> *Seeded (no inbound this week):* "Earlier digests called the federal appeal
> only 'the pending federal appeal'. Why does this one print its number?"

The number was never a secret. It was left out because the publish pipeline's
mechanical blocklist refused it on every public repo, and a digest carrying it
would have stopped the whole Friday push. On 2026-09-26 the operator ruled the
number fine to publish on this drive's pages, and on 2026-09-30 the pipeline
scoped the bar to match: this drive's repo may carry it, and every other public
repo still may not. So the digests now cite the appeal the way they cite the
district-court case — by number, with the Entry IDs that let anyone check it.
