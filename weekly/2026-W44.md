## 5. One reader question, answered

> *Seeded (no inbound this week):* "§ 3 shows 45 theorems removed from the
> operational axis. Does that mean proofs failed?"

No. A removed line means a statement no longer stands in that form, and most
of these were rewritten. This week's rounds tightened the definitions under
four synthetic examples. For instance, a withdrawal now records the version
that declared it. A theorem stated over the old definition no longer says the
same thing, so it was restated, and 29 of the 37 distinct names came back under
the same name. The other 8 did not return under those names. Attack theorems
went the same way, because each one is written against the definitions it
attacks. The red rounds in § 1 are a separate matter: an attack landed, and
that finding stays on the record for the next round to answer. Git keeps every
removed statement, so a reader can compare the before and after of each one.
