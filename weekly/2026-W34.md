## 5. One reader question, answered

> *(Seeded from this week's work — not an inbound.)* Your last digest said a
> donation page was telling people the channel was open when it isn't. It's
> fixed now. What stops the next version of that bug?

The honest answer is that a fix and a *structural* fix are different things, and
only the second one counts. The page was not lying on purpose: it composed the
words "Status: live" from the **drive window** — 2026-06-01 to 2026-12-01, which
is genuinely open — while the **money channel** was foreclosed, which is a
different fact entirely. Two true inputs, one false sentence. Patching the
sentence would have left the next surface free to make the same mistake, so the
change made on 2026-08-16 was to **derive the channel half of that status line
from the shared data hub** that already carried the foreclosure, on both sites,
rather than from the calendar. The same change also stopped both pages
hand-writing the fourth bucket's name, which is why a stale label disappeared in
the same pass. That is the general shape of every fix this drive will claim
credit for: remove the *opportunity* for the two halves to disagree, do not
just correct today's disagreement. What remains unautomated — and is stated
plainly rather than glossed — is that nothing forces a session to check the live
page afterward. This digest checks it by hand, every week, because a committed
fix is not a live one.
