## 5. One reader question, answered

> *Seeded (no inbound this week):* "§ 3 says 106 new theorems on one axis.
> How would I check a figure like that?"

Every count in § 3 is a difference between two snapshots of the source tree.
Take the last commit before the window opens and the last commit before it
closes; on each, list every top-level `theorem` declaration under one axis's
directory as file plus name; the new theorems are the names present at the end
and not the start, and the removed ones the reverse. The two commit hashes are
printed in § 3, so the result is fixed — anyone with the source, or with a
mirror refreshed from those commits, gets the same numbers. A rename counts as
one removed and one new, which is why removals are reported rather than netted
out. The same method checked W37's figures from the outside and found one too
high; that correction is now public in W37 itself.
