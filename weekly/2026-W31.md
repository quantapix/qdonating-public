## 5. One reader question, answered

> *Seeded from this week's § 6 correction.* "Earlier digests cited the First
> Circuit appeal by its docket number; now every digest says 'pending federal
> appeal.' Why did already-posted digests change?"

Because the publish pipeline's redaction gate hard-bars that docket number from
the public mirror, and the source digests were the last place still carrying
it. The W29 and W30 Friday refreshes both **aborted at the gate** on exactly
that token — the gate did its job — and the mirror copies were repaired by hand
while the source files kept the number, leaving source and mirror divergent.
This week the eight source digests (W23–W30) were conformed to the citation
shape the public mirrors already used. Two things did **not** change: every
Document and Entry ID was kept — each § 4 entry remains independently checkable
against the court's own records — and the docket number itself remains exactly
where it belongs, in the federal record. This is a citation-form fix, not a
content change.
